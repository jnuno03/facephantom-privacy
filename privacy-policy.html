Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Short version: FacePhantom processes camera, photo, and video data on your device. We do not operate a cloud backend for this app. Your media, email, and named-face hashes stay on this device unless you export or share them yourself.

1. Who we are

This policy describes how FacePhantom (“the app,” “we”) handles information. FacePhantom is a camera and gallery app that detects faces in photos and videos, lets you blur the faces you choose, and can remember people you name so they can be recognized later on the same device.

Developer contact: jnuno03@gmail.com

2. Information the app uses

FacePhantom may use the following information, stored locally on your device:

  • Email address. You enter an email to keep this device’s gallery and named faces tied to that sign-in. It is stored on the device (IndexedDB and local storage). It is not sent to a FacePhantom server — we do not operate an application server for accounts.
  • Photos and videos. Media you capture or import, including blurred exports you save, is stored on the device.
  • Named-face records. If you name a person, the app stores a one-way hash of a numeric face descriptor, the name you typed, and which account it belongs to. We do not store a crop of the face or the raw descriptor after hashing.
  • Session state. The app remembers that you are signed in until you log out.

Face detection runs in memory on the device. Live camera frames and temporary face descriptors are used to detect and match faces and are not uploaded to us.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, on FacePhantom servers:

  • Your original or blurred photos or videos
  • Face images, face embeddings, or face hashes
  • Precise location, contacts, or microphone audio (the camera capture path does not record audio)
  • Payment card details (the current app does not process payments)
  • Advertising identifiers for third-party ads (the current app does not show third-party ads)

We do not sell your information.

4. How we use information

  • To detect faces and let you choose which ones to blur or cover
  • To recognize people you have named, only against the face book for the email signed in on this device
  • To keep photos, videos, and named faces for that email on this device
  • To restore your session until you log out

Another email signed in on the same device cannot see your gallery or named faces. The hashing method is the same for every account; isolation is which records that email is allowed to load.

5. Face data

FacePhantom uses on-device machine learning to find faces and, if you have named people, to compare a live descriptor to stored hashes. Named-face hashes are not photographs. They are used only to match people you enrolled on that account. You can delete named faces in the app, or delete all local data in Settings.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera. To show a live preview, detect faces, and capture photos or videos.
  • Photos / storage (as provided by the OS or browser). To import media from your library and to save exports you choose to keep or share.

You can deny camera access in system or browser settings. The app cannot capture without that permission.

7. Third parties

On first use, FacePhantom may download publicly available face-detection model files from a content delivery network (jsDelivr) so detection can run on your device. Those downloads do not include your photos, videos, email, or face hashes.

If you install FacePhantom from Google Play, Google may process data under Google’s Privacy Policy.

If you share or save a file to another app (Photos, Messages, social apps, and so on), that destination’s policy applies to the file you send.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on the device until you delete it, clear site data, uninstall the app, or use Clear all local data in Settings. Logging out keeps media and named faces on the device but stops using that email until you sign in again.

9. Children

FacePhantom is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on a FacePhantom server. Photos you take may include children; that media remains on your device unless you export it.

10. Security

Because processing is on-device, your media is not transmitted to us. Protect access to your device with the screen lock provided by your operating system. No method of storage is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy if the app’s behavior changes (for example, if cloud accounts, ads, or payments are added). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
